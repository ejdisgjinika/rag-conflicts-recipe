## Recipe dataset

The accompanying paper cites
[Food.com – Recipes and Reviews](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/irkaal/foodcom-recipes-and-reviews)
by Kaggle user `irkaal` as its data source. The source data card lists
[CC0: Public Domain](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
The processed CSV files used by the experiment pipeline are in `data/`;
see the [data documentation](data/README.md) for their schema and use.
